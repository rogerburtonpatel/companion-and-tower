\note{\textbf{Tactic surface.} The rewrite finishes well below the paco baseline
at both scopes: in \code{theories/}, $16 \to 11$ names ($-31\%$), $118 \to 66$
lines ($-44\%$), $4\,181 \to 2\,283$ characters ($-45.4\%$); across the whole
development, $4\,586 \to 2\,545$ characters ($-44.5\%$). The first port went the
wrong way on every one of them. Ten tactic families disappear from
\code{theories/} outright between the two ports; three of them
(\code{inf_closed_auto}, \code{monauto}, \code{tower induction}) went because the
library's own versions became good enough to reach the files that needed them,
and the \code{to_mon} family collapsed into a single shared driver.}

\note{\textbf{The downstream developments now cost less than they did under
paco.} \code{extra/} carried $405$ characters of coinduction-driving Ltac at the
paco checkpoint, $4\,217$ after the first port, and $262$ now. The reason is that
the per-functor \code{to_mon} blocks, which were the whole of the regression,
are gone: each development states one \code{reflexivity} lemma and registers it,
and a single driver in \code{Basics/Utils.v} does the folding. This is the
clearest single piece of evidence that the first port's cost was implementation
and not theory.}

\note{\textbf{Characters move about three times as far as lines.} Proof lines
fall $4.1\%$ across the whole development while proof characters fall $11.8\%$;
in \code{theories/}, $1.6\%$ against $8.4\%$. Enhanced coinduction did not mainly
delete proof lines, it made the surviving lines shorter. This is the argument for
reporting both, and it is a finding the paper would have missed on a line count
alone.}

\note{\textbf{The two halves moved in opposite directions.} \code{extra/} gained
more proof reduction than \code{theories/} did, and also paid the most tactic
surface to adopt the library. The downstream users had the most to gain and the
most to pay.}

\note{\textbf{Build time} (\Cref{tab:timing}). The first port cost $+25.6\%$
against paco; the rewrite recovers it to $+4.1\%$. Diagnosed causes of the first
port's regression, none inherent to enhanced coinduction: the port dropped the
explicit \code{eauto} depth annotations the paco code carried, leaving the
default depth whose last two levels go to the standard library's boolean
machinery; \code{inv} gained a \code{try easy} and is called at $1118$ sites; and
$33$ sites changed \code{hinduction ... before r} to \code{... before E},
reverting the whole context instead of the tail.}
