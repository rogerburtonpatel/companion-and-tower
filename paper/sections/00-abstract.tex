Coinductive reasoning is central to mechanized semantics, and the Rocq Proof
Assistant offers several abstractions to support it. Parameterized coinduction
(paco) underlies the Interaction Trees (ITrees) library, while enhanced
coinduction, most recently formulated through the tower induction principle over
the companion, promises greater simplicity and better compositionality of up-to
techniques. We evaluate enhanced coinduction at scale by refactoring the ITrees
library from paco to a tower-based enhanced coinduction library. The exercise
revealed weaknesses in the existing enhanced coinduction implementation and
prompted fully rewriting it. The new library eliminates the OCaml plugin in
favor of a pure Gallina and Ltac formulation, reduces the codebase by 62\%,
extends documentation and test coverage, and fixes several latent bugs along the
way. Re-integrating the rewritten library into ITrees validates the approach.
\gap{``Coinductive proofs shrink by N\% in lines of code, and individual lemmas

These results suggest that tower-based enhanced coinduction is not only a
theoretical simplification but a practical improvement for large coinductive
developments. We describe the design decisions behind the rewrite and draw
lessons for coinduction library design in Rocq.
