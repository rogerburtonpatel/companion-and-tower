\note{\textbf{One number the talk does not have, and should.} Total size, every
non-blank comment-free line counted once, goes $20\,871 \to 20\,455 \to 20\,156$
across the whole development, which is $-2.0\%$ at the first port and $-3.4\%$
after the rewrite. It never rose. That is worth stating because ``the tactic
surface tripled'' invites the reader to assume the development grew, and it did
not.}

\subsection{Other perks}

\code{icauto} is user-facing, previously internal only.

\code{monauto} is user facing, previously nonexistent (but was in paco).

\gap{The two before/after pairs from slide 88. The first collapses six lines of
\code{apply inf_closed_*} plumbing into \code{tower induction.}; the second
collapses a five-line monotonicity proof into \code{Proof. monauto. Qed.}
Supporting figure: \code{monauto} discharges $17$ of the $18$ monotonicity
statements in ITrees, and \code{tower induction} is used at $22$ sites while
ITrees contains \emph{zero} calls to \code{icauto}, because the obligation never
reaches the user.}
